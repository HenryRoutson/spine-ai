# Legal Considerations

## R-01 — Non-compliant
MediFlow AI collects user data under a single consent checkbox, which does not explicitly separate distinct processing purposes.

The company uses a single checkbox for Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, covering multiple purposes without explicit consent for each distinct purpose.

**Estimated exposure:** $500000

## R-02 — Non-compliant
MediFlow AI collects health data without a separate, affirmative opt-in consent recorded and time-stamped.

Health data is collected under a general consent mechanism, lacking a separate, affirmative opt-in consent.

**Estimated exposure:** $500000

## R-03 — Non-compliant
MediFlow AI encrypts data in transit using TLS 1.3 but does not encrypt data at rest.

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3, but encryption at rest is not implemented.

**Estimated exposure:** $500000

## R-04 — Non-compliant
MediFlow AI stores and processes EEA resident data in the US without an approved transfer mechanism.

EEA resident data is stored in AWS us-east-1 (Northern Virginia) without segregation or an approved transfer mechanism.

**Estimated exposure:** $500000

## R-05 — Compliant
MediFlow AI provides a clear explanation of logic and a route to human review for urgent cases flagged by its AI system.

The company offers a clear explanation of AI logic and routes urgent cases for human review.

## R-06 — Non-compliant
MediFlow AI's incident-response runbook specifies breach reporting within five business days, exceeding the 72-hour requirement.

The company's breach reporting timeline exceeds the 72-hour requirement.

**Estimated exposure:** $500000
