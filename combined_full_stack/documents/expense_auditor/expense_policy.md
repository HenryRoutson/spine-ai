# Acme Corp Travel & Expense Policy (FY2025)

## Category limits (per trip, USD)

| Category | Daily limit | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| Meals | 75 | Alcohol not reimbursable |
| Lodging | 220 | Pre-approval required above 300 |
| Ground transport | 120 | Rideshare OK; luxury car service excluded |
| Airfare | economy only | Business class requires VP approval |
| Client entertainment | 150 | Receipt + attendee list required |

## Receipt requirements

- Itemized receipt required for any line over $25.
- Personal items on a mixed receipt must be excluded before submission.

## Non-compliant examples

- Tips above 20% without written justification.
- Mini-bar charges.
- Upgrades purchased at check-in without pre-approval.
