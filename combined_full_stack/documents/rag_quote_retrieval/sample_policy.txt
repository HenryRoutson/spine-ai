EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK — TERMINATION AND REFUNDS

Section 1 — Termination
All employees must receive at least thirty (30) days written notice before involuntary termination without cause.
Severance pay equals two weeks of base salary per completed year of service when notice is not provided.

Section 2 — Subscriptions
Annual subscription refunds are available within thirty days of purchase when no more than ten percent of seats were used.

Section 3 — Parking
Downtown office parking assignments are managed by facilities and do not affect employment terms.
