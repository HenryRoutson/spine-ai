# Credit Agreement — Northwind Holdings

Borrower: Northwind Holdings LLC
Facility: $40,000,000 senior secured revolving credit
Closing: 12 March 2024

Covenants
- Maximum net leverage 4.50x, stepping down to 4.00x after four full fiscal quarters.
- Minimum interest coverage 2.00x.
- No restricted payments while a default is continuing.

Change of control
A Change of Control occurs if any person other than the Sponsor acquires more than 50% of voting equity.
