# Legal Considerations

## R-01 — Non-compliant
MediFlow AI collects user data with a single checkbox consent covering multiple purposes, failing to obtain explicit consent for each distinct purpose.

The company uses a single checkbox for consent, which does not meet the requirement for explicit consent for each distinct purpose of data processing.

**Estimated exposure:** $500000

## R-02 — Non-compliant
MediFlow AI does not obtain a separate, affirmative opt-in consent for health data, which is required for processing such sensitive information.

The company collects health data without a separate, affirmative opt-in consent, violating the requirement for handling health data.

**Estimated exposure:** $500000

## R-03 — Non-compliant
MediFlow AI encrypts data in transit using TLS 1.3 but fails to encrypt data at rest, which is required for personal data protection.

While data in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.3, data at rest is not encrypted, failing to meet the encryption requirements for personal data.

**Estimated exposure:** $500000

## R-04 — Non-compliant
MediFlow AI stores and processes EEA residents' data in the US without an approved transfer mechanism, violating data localization requirements.

The company hosts EEA residents' data in the US without an approved transfer mechanism, breaching the requirement to store and process such data exclusively within the EEA.

**Estimated exposure:** $500000

## R-05 — Compliant
MediFlow AI's automated triage system provides explanations and routes urgent cases for human review, but lacks a documented bias assessment.

The company's automated system offers explanations and human review for urgent cases, meeting the requirement for transparency and human intervention.

## R-06 — Non-compliant
MediFlow AI's incident response plan requires breach reporting within five business days, exceeding the 72-hour requirement.

The company's breach reporting timeline of five business days exceeds the 72-hour requirement for notifying supervisory authorities.

**Estimated exposure:** $500000
