Methodology & caveats

  • Filter applied: started from the full 52-tool research pass, kept the 10 tools scoring ≥7/10 similarity to the brief, then removed n8n and added Relevance AI (relevanceai.com — 6/10 similarity, below the cutoff) by explicit request, for 11 tools total.
  • Research: parallel web-search passes prioritizing primary sources (company blogs, press releases, TechCrunch/Bloomberg/SiliconANGLE) over third-party estimators (GetLatka, Sacra) where both existed. Figures current as of August 2026.
  • Similarity score (0–10): scored by hand against the brief. This set is dominated by visual/no-code AI-agent and LLM-workflow builders with hosting and a typed/code-export story — the closest analogs to "n8n + Lovable for AI engineering."
  • Fitted curve: ordinary least-squares regression of log10(valuation) on log10(ARR) across the 8 plotted tools (real figures where disclosed, peer-modeled where not) — valuation ≈ 16.6 × ARR0.83, R² = 0.47. With only 8 points and 3 of them peer-modeled on one axis, treat this as a rough visual trend, not a precise pricing model.
  • Total valuation: straight sum of disclosed/credibly-reported valuations among these 11 tools (4 have one: Flowise, StackAI, Relevance AI, Dify AI). Removing the low-similarity mega-caps (Anthropic, OpenAI, Cursor, etc.) that dominated the original full-list total is what keeps this in the billions rather than trillions.
  • Proportional (overlap) valuation: Σ(valuation × similarity ÷ 10) across those same 4 tools.
  • Modeled figures (chart only): for tools with only ARR or only valuation disclosed, the missing axis is imputed using the ~25.4× median valuation:ARR multiple observed across the full 52-tool research set. A rough peer proxy, not a researched number — exists only so the tool can appear on the scatter plot, and is excluded from the total/proportional valuation figures. Flagged with a dashed ring on the chart and "modeled" in the table.
  • Excluded from the plot (no usable figure on either axis): Langflow (now part of DataStax, no independent figures), Wordware, and BuildShip — all still appear in the table.
