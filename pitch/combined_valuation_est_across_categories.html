What this changes in the pitch
The current deck prices Spine against back-end × consumer no-code agent builders, where Relevance AI leads at ~$13M ARR and Dify is the closest product at $180M. That cell is real — and it is the smallest of the four. Front-end × consumer is led by Lovable at $500M ARR / $13.3B. Back-end × developer is led by n8n at $5.2B (SAP, May 2026) and LangChain at $1.25B. Spine’s graduated complexity (consumer Run → non-technical builder → developer typed pipelines + code export) is built to occupy all four cells, which is why a Relevance-only comp set understates the ceiling. Methodology & caveats

  • Why four cells. Spine is not one product. Consumer mode is a Run/marketplace surface (back-end × consumer). The landing-page agent and non-technical builder are vibe-coding (front-end × consumer). Typed structures, pipeline, testing, logging, and multi-language export are back-end × developer. Hosted inference UIs, custom LLM providers, and teams are the enterprise/developer front-end. Pricing against only Relevance/Dify is pricing one cell.
  • What replaced the imputed point. A missing or unreliable figure becomes a band along the uncertain axis; both uncertain → a box. The regression uses each band's geometric centre and weights it by 1 / (1 + log₁₀(width_x) + log₁₀(width_y)).
  • Three reasons a figure becomes a band. reported — good sources disagree, or the company gave a floor and a target (Replit $150M vs $1B goal; Relevance $38M Latka vs $120M Finro). stale — one solid figure old enough that "now" is a range, upper bound = cohort growth (Bolt $40M from Mar 2025; n8n $40M from Jul 2025). modeled — nothing on that axis; band spans the peer-multiple IQR (17×–29× used to fill, observed IQR 16.7× / 23.2× / 27.4× on 15 dual-axis comps).
  • The $13M ARR line. Relevance AI is the highest-ARR name in the no-code AI-agent box, not in AI engineering. Finro Q1 2026 still has it at ~$13M / ~$120M (9.5×). n8n ($5.2B), LangChain ($1.25B), and Lovable ($13.3B) are not in that box.
  • n8n. Accel Series C Oct 2025 at $2.5B on $40M ARR (Sacra, Jul 2025). SAP strategic investment 12 May 2026 values it at $5.2B and did not publish a new ARR — so ARR is a stale band $40–130M, not a point. Do not quote 130× as a comparable multiple.
  • Lovable. $400M Series C at $13.3B on 12 Aug 2026 (TechCrunch / company blog). $500M annualised run-rate in June, tracking ~$600M by end of August. Run-rate, not audited revenue. Enterprise is still a small slice (~$20M of the earlier $400M ARR print).
  • Cursor is a ceiling, not a comp. $60B is an all-stock SpaceX deal price announced 16 Jun 2026, not a financing mark, and Cursor is an editor rather than a pipeline host. Similarity 5/10. The fit without Cursor is almost the same (val ≈ 19.1 × ARR1.03).
  • Products inside bigger companies. v0, Base44, and Make have no standalone valuation — vertical modeled bands/boxes. StackAI is an acquisition price ($75M, Asana, May 2026), a different instrument from a primary round.
  • The fit. val ≈ 20.0 × ARR1.01 (R² = 0.94, n = 23). Exponent ≈ 1 means a roughly constant ~20× multiple across four orders of magnitude. R² is inflated by the log–log scale and by Cursor/Lovable anchoring the top right. Treat as a visual trend, not a pricing model.
  • Similarity (0–10). Hand-scored against Spine: typed schemas, hosted runs, code export, eval/testing, share/marketplace, vibe-coding for non-technical users. Dify and n8n are 9; Gumloop/Windmill/Vellum/BAML/Lovable are 8; Relevance is 6 (demo-ware, but the pitch's cited leader).
  • Sources. Primary: company blogs and BusinessWire/TechCrunch/Bloomberg. Secondary: Sacra, ARR Club, Finro, Latka (flagged when they are the only number). Research pass 13 Aug 2026.
